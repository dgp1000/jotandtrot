Everything a trip needs, in one place

Made for planning with friends and family — everyone sees the same trip, live.

✨

Scout any area

Point the map anywhere and hit “Scout this area.” Ideas stream onto the map one by one — sights, food, viewpoints — ready to add to your trip.

⚡

Auto-build your schedule

Collected a pile of ideas? One tap slots them into your days into a sensible schedule you can rearrange freely.

🗳️

Vote as a crew

Share a trip with a code or the share sheet. Everyone suggests, votes with a thumbs up or down, and sees what’s new since they last looked.

🏨

Hotels that span nights

Pick a hotel and it flows across your stay, check-in to check-out — with per-night prices in your budget. Splitting the stay across two hotels? Handled.

💶

Budgets per day

Every stop can carry a price, and each day totals itself up, so nobody is surprised at the end.

✈️

Works offline

Your itinerary is cached on your phone, so it's there in airplane mode or on a metro with no signal. Push notifications tell you when someone adds stops.

How it works

From blank map to shared itinerary in minutes.

1

Start a trip

Name it, set your dates and destination.

2

Scout & search

Scout areas for ideas or search for specific places, scoped to the map you're looking at.

3

Invite your crew

Share a code. Everyone adds ideas and votes on what makes the cut.

4

Build the plan

Auto-build the schedule, tweak it together, and take it with you — even offline.

Easy on the eyes

A full night mode — dark map tiles included — for planning tomorrow from tonight's hotel bed.
